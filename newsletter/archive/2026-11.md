## Follow-on Funding from SCRC-Funded Research

*Suggested Curated.co category: Updates from the SCRC*

We are delighted to share that researchers previously supported by Sussex Cancer Research Centre (SCRC) and Sussex Cancer Fund awards have secured major external follow-on funding to advance their projects:

- **Dr Antony Oliver — Blood Cancer UK (£29,991):** Following their August 2025 SCRC Pump-Priming Award (*"A new therapeutic option in acute myeloid leukaemia"*), [Antony Oliver](https://sussexcancer.org/directory/antony-oliver) has secured external funding from **Blood Cancer UK** of **£29,991** ([view award details](https://bloodcancer.org.uk/research/research-projects/developing-a-new-treatment-for-an-aggressive-form-of-acute-myeloid-leukaemia-aml/)).
- **Dr Frances Pearl — Cancer Research UK & UKRI Proof of Concept:** Following their Inaugural SCRC PhD Studentship (2025) (*"AI solutions to guide treatment strategies for Cancers of Unknown Primaries (CUPs)"*), [Frances Pearl](https://sussexcancer.org/directory/frances-pearl) has secured external funding from **Cancer Research UK** — *Strategy for the findability and accessibility of CRUK data* ([view details](https://profiles.sussex.ac.uk/p140897-frances-pearl/grants)); and **UKRI Proof of Concept** — *Pearl AI – DependANT* ([view details](https://www.ukri.org/news/48-projects-backed-to-turn-cutting-edge-research-into-businesses/)).
- **Dr Luke Yates — Academy of Medical Sciences (£125,000):** Following their 2024/25 SCRC Pump-Priming Award (*"PP4 as a novel target in the development of new therapies for ovarian cancer"*), Dr Luke Yates has secured external funding from **Academy of Medical Sciences** of **£125,000** ([view award details](https://www.sussex.ac.uk/broadcast/read/70537)).

Explore all SCRC-funded projects and their follow-on funding on the [SCRC Funding page](https://sussexcancer.org/funding).
