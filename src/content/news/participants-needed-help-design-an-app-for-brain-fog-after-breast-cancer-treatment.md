date: 2026-03-30
excerpt: "This study involves the collaborative design and development of a mobile app prototype specifically created to help breast cancer survivors manage cognitive impairments like \"brain fog.\" Eligibility: Participants must be adults who have completed breast cancer treatment and are currently experiencing difficulties with memory, concentration, or processing speed. Commitment: Volunteers will participate in up to four flexible online interviews or focus groups to review app features and share their personal experiences. Impact: The feedback gathered in each session will directly shape and refine the app’s design to ensure it effectively meets the needs of its users."

<p>Get in Touch with Maryam, PhD Researcher: <a href="mailto:Maryam.ibrar@strath.ac.uk">Maryam.ibrar@strath.ac.uk</a></p><p>Co-design and development of a mobile app prototype for the management of cancer-related cognitive impairment in individuals with breast cancer.</p><p>Recruitment criteria:</p><p>- Aged 18 years or over</p><p>- Have completed breast cancer treatment</p><p>- Currently experiencing cancer-related cognitive difficulties (e.g. brain fog, memory, concentration, attention, or processing speed difficulties)</p><p>Study involvement:</p><p>- Participation in up to four online sessions, consisting of either one-to-one interviews or small focus groups</p><p>- Sessions will be scheduled flexibly, including evenings and weekends where required</p><p>- Participants will be shown examples of proposed app features and design concepts</p><p>- Participants will be asked to share their experiences, opinions, and feedback on these features</p><p>Feedback from each session will be used to inform and refine&nbsp;the app design for subsequent sessions</p><p>Your Voice Matters.
