<p>People with lived experience of cancer spend many hours in hospital waiting rooms, often experiencing anxiety and stress. <strong>Sussex Cancer Fund</strong> are sponsoring the project <strong>‘Words from the Waiting Room’</strong>, which will offer a space for patients and/or friends and family of patients who have spent time in the oncology waiting room to explore their experiences creatively.</p><p>In a free two-hour workshop, Deborah and Karina will use writing prompts to help participants explore their own stories, with opportunities to use collaged words for those who feel inhibited by writing.</p><p>Book your workshop in <a href="https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/words-from-the-waiting-room-free-brighton-writing-workshop-tickets-1268712867339?aff=erelpanelorg">Brighton</a></p><p>Book your workshop in <a href="https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/words-from-the-waiting-room-free-hastings-writing-workshop-tickets-1268752866979?utm_experiment=test_share_listing&amp;aff=ebdsshios">Hastings</a></p>
