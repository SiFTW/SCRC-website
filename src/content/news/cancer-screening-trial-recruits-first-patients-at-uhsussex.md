<p>University Hospitals Sussex has recruited its first patients into a national clinical trial investigating a simple, less invasive method to improve early detection and monitoring of people at risk of oesophageal cancer.</p><p>The trial, known as BEST4, is exploring whether a ‘sponge on a string’ test could replace some of the routine endoscopies currently used to monitor patients with Barrett’s oesophagus (BO).</p><p><a href="https://www.uhsussex.nhs.uk/news/sponge-on-a-string-cancer-screening-trial-recruits-first-patients-at-uhsussex/" target="_blank">Read more</a></p>
