<p>University Hospitals Sussex NHS Foundation Trust (UHSussex) is recruiting a Professor/Associate Professor of Clinical Oncology, a key new role designed to advance world-class cancer research in collaboration with the Sussex Cancer Research Centre (SCRC) and Brighton and Sussex Medical School.<br><br>This appointment represents an exciting opportunity to lead a programme of independent and collaborative research within one of the most research-active NHS Trusts in the South East. The successful candidate will contribute to the development of new clinical trials, mentor future researchers, and work alongside SCRC partners to translate groundbreaking discoveries into better treatments for patients.</p><p>
