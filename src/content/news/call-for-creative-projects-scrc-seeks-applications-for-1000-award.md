<p><strong>Apply Now for the SCRC Creative Engagement Award (Up to £1000)</strong></p><p><strong>Applications close on 31st November 2024.</strong></p><p>The Sussex Cancer Research Centre is offering an exciting funding opportunity for creative projects that build meaningful connections between people with lived experience of cancer and the cutting-edge cancer research happening at SCRC.</p><p>The <strong>SCRC Creative Engagement Award</strong> provides up to <strong>£1000</strong> for innovative projects using art and creative approaches to engage the local community with cancer research. This award is open to all creative forms, and applicants do not need to be affiliated with universities or hospitals.</p><p>We encourage applications from creative professionals or those currently training in the arts. Projects should aim to inspire reflection, conversation, and engagement with cancer research, with a focus on equitable and diverse community involvement. Traditional formats such as seminars or workshops are unlikely to be competitive; instead, we are looking for fresh, impactful creative solutions.</p><p><strong>What’s Included:</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Up to £1000</strong> in funding for your creative project</p></li><li><p>Full support from the SCRC management team to help with the delivery and facilitate engagement with cancer researchers</p></li><li><p>The opportunity to have your work featured in SCRC newsletters, social media, and at future events</p></li></ul><p>To apply, visit <a href="https://sussexcancer.org/funding">funding — Sussex Cancer Research Centre</a></p>
